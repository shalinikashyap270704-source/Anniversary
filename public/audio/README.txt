===========================================================
🎵 AUDIO FOLDER - HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN AUDIO & MUSIC
===========================================================

You can easily add your own romantic background music or anniversary song here!

Recommended filenames:
1. background_music.mp3  -> Plays soft, romantic background music throughout the website
2. anniversary_song.mp3  -> Special song choice

💡 TIPS:
- Standard format: .mp3 (or .wav, .m4a, .ogg)
- When a music file named "background_music.mp3" is added to this folder, the website will automatically play it!
- The audio can be muted/unmuted anytime by clicking the Volume icon (🔊) in the top-right corner.
